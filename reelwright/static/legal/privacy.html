Reelwright Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Reelwright is provided by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Reelwright is a video editor and clip-publishing app that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information Reelwright handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: Reelwright has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your information with us. Your videos, transcripts, settings and account tokens stay on your computer, and are sent only to the services you choose to connect, when you use a feature that needs them.

1. Information stored on your computer

Reelwright stores the following in its data folder (on Windows, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Reelwright for the installed app, or the data folder when run from source):

  • Your media and edits: videos you import or receive from OBS, thumbnails, rendered clips, cover images, music you add, transcripts (speech-to-text), detected audio, scene, on-screen activity and chat signals, and your clip edits.
  • Settings and credentials: your Anthropic API key, the OAuth tokens for the YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts you connect, OBS WebSocket passwords you enter, and the pairing token for receiving clips from other PCs. These are stored unencrypted in files that, by default, only your user account can read; protect your computer accordingly.
  • Learning memory: to improve clip picks, Reelwright keeps a record of your feedback — clips you rate, publish or delete — including each clip's title, up to 1,500 characters of its transcript, measured traits (length, loudness, pacing), your edit choices, and the view and like counts of your published posts. This record is kept even after you delete the clip or project, so the lessons are not lost. You can export it, import it or delete all of it at any time (Virality Lab → “What Reelwright has learned”).
  • Diagnostics: a local event log (events.log, rotated at 2 MB) and a record of Claude API usage (token counts and estimated cost per feature, without prompt contents).

Speech transcription (Whisper), on-screen activity analysis and face detection run entirely on your computer. Reelwright contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and sends no usage data to us.

3. Google API Services: Limited Use disclosure

Reelwright's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to provide the features you use in Reelwright — publishing your videos and showing and learning from their performance — and is not transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by people. Reelwright uses YouTube API Services; by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How long information is kept

Everything stays on your computer until you delete it. Deleting a project removes its media, transcript and clips; the learning memory is kept until you reset it. Uninstalling the app does not delete the data folder; delete that folder to remove everything. Data already sent to a third-party service (for example, a published video) is kept by that service under its own policy.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect any account on Reelwright's Publish page; this deletes its stored tokens from your computer.
  • Revoke access at the source as well: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Meta under Settings → Apps and websites.
  • Remove your API key in Settings to stop all Claude requests.
  • Reset, export or import the learning memory in Virality Lab.
  • Turn off the OBS network receiver at any time on the OBS link page.

6. Security

Reelwright's main interface can only be reached from your own computer. The optional network receiver for OBS clips accepts only signed, time-limited requests, can only create new projects, and never publishes the clips it receives publicly. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and your credentials are stored on your computer, so keep your computer and accounts protected.

7. Children

Reelwright is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Social platforms set their own minimum ages.

8. Content about other people

Videos you process may show or mention other people. You are responsible for having the right to process and publish that content; see the Terms of Use.
